General

Categories

Rename any category with ✎, built-in ones included: its transactions follow. The ones you added (highlighted) can be deleted with ✕, and a renamed built-in one gets its name back with ↺. You can also add one while editing a transaction (+ New category… at the end of the list).

Live prices

Crypto prices (CoinGecko) and exchange rates work without a key. For stocks and ETFs, get a free API key from finnhub.io (US stocks) or alphavantage.co (more exchanges, 25 requests/day) and paste it here. You can always type prices in by hand instead.

Finnhub's free plan has no TSX prices (VFV, XEQT…). With this key, Canadian holdings are looked up on Alpha Vantage and the others on your provider above.

While the app is open: stocks every 15 minutes when the markets are open (9:30 to 16:00, weekdays) and once after the close, crypto every 15 minutes, exchange rates every 6 hours. Alpha Vantage's free key allows 25 lookups a day in all, so Canadian holdings are updated less often (with 3 of them, about every hour and a half). CDRs keep the price from your statements.
